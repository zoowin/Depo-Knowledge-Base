## Strategic decision

June and July show that customers respond to a clear product benefit, price advantage, defined body zone, or timely offer. Abstract brand stories, broad routine theory, and review-only content generate less revenue. August therefore gives every email one clear Hero and answers the customer's question first: **What will this do for me, and why should I buy it now?**

- **One short segmented sale.** Aug. 17–18 recreates the proven 2025 win-back structure: Yearly Customers receive 25% OFF and Yearly Repeat Customers receive 20% OFF. Each date uses one master creative with two audience-specific offer versions, deployed as four Klaviyo campaigns in total.
- **Fourteen creative sends, twelve primary product Hero targets.** Aug. 5 uses MOP as the education Hero with Peptide Activation Trio shown only as the coordinated next choice; Aug. 14 uses Peptide Activation Trio as the complete-program conversion Hero. The Summer Sale uses an event Hero for launch and final-day reminder. Every active product Hero target appears once.
- **Every email has one customer promise.** Product history, brand rationale, and internal formulation stories may support the promise but may never become the lead.
- **Supporting products must finish the same job.** Each recommendation is either the next routine step, a daily/alternate-night companion, or a clearly explained choice—not a generic product grid.
- **Classic Eye Micro-dart stays in the plan.** It closes August on Aug. 31, 31 days after its Jul. 31 Hero send. Forehead Patch returns as the focused alternate-night recommendation in the Aug. 24 MPS email; confirm air-shipment receipt and sellable inventory before scheduling.

### August copy rule

Every draft must establish the following within the Hero section:

1. **Customer outcome:** what she can expect to look or feel different.
2. **Practical advantage:** what becomes easier, more targeted, or more complete.
3. **Purchase reason:** verified saving, cost per use, product format, or why this product fits the problem now.

Background science appears only after those three points are clear. “Why we made it,” founder stories, and company milestones are excluded unless they directly prove a customer benefit.

## Compliance and draft requirements

- All campaigns use the MEL structure and must be drafted from the standard output template.
- Hero copy must lead with the customer's outcome or practical advantage. Limit brand/product-origin narrative to one supporting sentence only when it proves a customer benefit.
- Do not use review roundups or customer stories as the main narrative unless a verified quote materially answers a product objection.
- Both older Forehead drafts remain local HOLD files only. Do not activate or schedule them without a separate inventory decision. The active Aug. 24 MPS email may recommend Forehead Patch once the air shipment is physically received, checked in, and sellable.
- The Aug. 5 MOP email keeps MOP as the Hero and shows Peptide Activation Trio only in the lower recommendation area. Both Aug. 5 and Aug. 14 must reconfirm MOP inventory, the bundle's $89 sale price and $135 regular price, exact included products, and whether the complimentary M3K gift remains active. Never state the live remaining gift quantity. If the gift has ended, use each draft's complete gift-ended replacement block.
- The former Aug. 10 Night Eye Patch draft is local HOLD material only. The active Aug. 10 Body Lotion campaign must remain single-product focused and use no unrelated face or eye recommendations.
- The Aug. 17–18 Summer Sale drafts require two complete audience versions: Yearly Customers at 25% OFF and Yearly Repeat Customers at 20% OFF. Use the Shopify `/discount/{CODE}?redirect=` format after the codes are confirmed. Do not imply that product-page markdowns and audience discounts stack unless Shopify testing confirms it.
- The Summer Sale follows the Alex Principle: explain that it is a private two-day customer offer, then state the subscriber's saving and deadline. Keep the explanation brief and customer-centered.
- The MOP campaign may say **topical**, **non-invasive**, and **appearance-level support**. Do not claim it replaces or equals professional micro-needling, or that it penetrates deeply.
- The Aug. 20 Eye Cream and Aug. 31 Micro-dart emails must clearly distinguish daily cream, hydration-focused hydrogel nights, and targeted Micro-dart nights. Do not imply that any format penetrates deeply or replaces a procedure.
- The Matriplex, M3K, MPS, and bundle campaigns must rely on their approved product cards only. Use support / smoother-looking / hydration language, not structural-change or guaranteed-result language.
- The Aug. 7 and Aug. 28 value emails may state only same-day verified live prices. Use plain product URLs unless a confirmed discount code is supplied.
- The Aug. 10 Body Lotion campaign requires the approved retinol note: PM use, sunscreen on exposed skin during the day, start 2–3 times per week if new or sensitive, and avoid pairing with strong exfoliating body products on the same area.
- Do not introduce reviews, clinical results, dermatologist endorsements, stock statements, discounts, or Labor Day promises unless a current approved asset or campaign decision is supplied.

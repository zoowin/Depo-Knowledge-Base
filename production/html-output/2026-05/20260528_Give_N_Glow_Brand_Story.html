Where 800 Bottles Went.

This is one of those things we don’t usually email about. But this spring, something happened we wanted you to know.

We had wrinkle serum we wanted to put into hands that don’t usually get to try this kind of skincare. Not sitting in our warehouse. Not heading to landfill. Hands.

That’s where Give n’ Glow came in — a Boston nonprofit with a network that gets quality skincare to women across the city who don’t usually have access to it. 800+ of our serums went out through them this spring.

It matters to us — and it couldn’t happen without you. Every order you place gives us the room to make choices like this one.

That’s the whole story. We just wanted you to know what your support makes possible.

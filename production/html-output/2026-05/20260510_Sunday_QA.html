Every week, our team gets the same questions in DMs, emails, and at events. Here are the five we hear most — and the honest answers, no marketing spin.

Q1
Why do my eyes show age before the rest of my face?

Three reasons stack up: the skin is 30% thinner, you blink around 15,000 times a day, and the orbital area has fewer oil glands — meaning less natural buffering. That’s why fine lines and shadows show up there first, even when the rest of your face still looks fine. An eye treatment isn’t vanity — it’s the highest-leverage square inch on your face.

Q2
Can I use peptides AND retinol together?

Yes — and they work better together than apart. Peptides build structure underneath; retinol speeds renewal on top. Order matters: peptide serum first (lighter, water-based), retinol second. And give it 6–8 weeks before judging — both work cumulatively, not overnight. Our upgraded M1 patches are designed for exactly this layering.

Q3
How do I know if my barrier is damaged?

Three quiet signs: tightness within 60 seconds of cleansing, products that suddenly sting when they didn’t before, and skin that looks dull no matter how much you hydrate. The fix isn’t more product — it’s less. Strip the routine down for 2–3 weeks until the barrier rebuilds. Then your actives will start working again.

Q4
Why did the routine that worked at 35 stop working at 50?

Two things shift. Skin renewal slows from ~28 days in your 20s to 45–60 days after 50. And your skin produces fewer natural lipids and less moisture year over year. So the moisturizer that “worked before” isn’t broken — your skin’s needs changed. The fix is stronger lipids (ceramides, cholesterol), targeted peptides, and patience to let actives accumulate.

Q5
Should I do MORE or LESS as I get older?

Less, but better. After 45, skin’s tolerance for layering drops — too many actives compete and can compromise the barrier you’re trying to support. A focused routine of 4–5 products with proven actives (peptides, ceramides, careful retinol, SPF) consistently outperforms a 10-step routine. It’s not about doing more. It’s about doing the right things, longer.

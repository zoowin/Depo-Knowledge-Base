# SMS Campaign: Memorial Sale Launch Alert

**Campaign:** Memorial Sale Launch
**Send Date:** 2026-05-20 (Wednesday)
**Send Time:** 11:00 AM ET（夹在 5/20 AM 9:00 + PM 5:00 两封 email 之间）
**Goal:** 增强 Memorial Launch day 触达 — SMS 抓 email 没第一时间打开的人，驱动当日订单 spike
**Target Audience:** Leon 现有 SMS segments（按以往 SMS 发送 segment 配置，无需新建）
**Tone:** 直接、紧迫、克制 — Depology 一贯的成熟调性，不要 spammy

---

## Strategy

Memorial Launch 是 5 月最大单日 revenue moment，2025 全天双发 email 跑出 $72k。SMS 在 11AM 中段触发，配合 9AM email + 5PM resend 形成 3 触点节奏。

核心信息：
1. **The Event:** Memorial Sale 已开
2. **The Offer:** 50% off sitewide
3. **The Action:** Click to shop
4. **The Time:** Through Mon 5/25

---

## SMS Copy Options

### Option 1: Direct Launch (Recommended)
*最简洁，直接传递 offer + link。*

**Copy:**
Depology: Memorial Sale just dropped — 50% off sitewide, no code. Through Monday at midnight. Shop: [Link]. Text STOP to unsub.

**Character Count:** ~135 chars (excluding link length)

### Option 2: Tease + Offer
*带一点 brand voice 的开场。*

**Copy:**
Depology: It's live — 50% off everything for Memorial weekend. No code needed. Ends Mon 11:59 PM ET. Shop now: [Link]. STOP to unsub.

**Character Count:** ~140 chars

### Option 3: Urgency-Forward
*强调倒计时。*

**Copy:**
Depology: Memorial 50% off is live for the next 5 days only. Sitewide. No code. Shop: [Link]. STOP to opt out.

**Character Count:** ~115 chars

---

## 推荐 Copy
**Option 1**（最直接，传达 launch + offer + 时限）

## 备注
- ⚠️ Smart Sending OFF（确保 SMS 触达，不被 Klaviyo 频率限制屏蔽）
- ⚠️ Link 用 Klaviyo 自动短链或 bit.ly，指向 `/pages/memorial-2026-sale` 落地页
- 📊 SMS 平均 CTR 15-25%，Memorial Launch 预期增量 revenue $3,000 - $6,000
- 🚨 5/20 是 3 触点天（AM email + 11AM SMS + PM email），确保 SMS Subscribers segment 已从 PM email 中排除（避免叠加疲劳）
- ✉️ MMS 可选：上传 Memorial 50% 主视觉一图，提升点击率

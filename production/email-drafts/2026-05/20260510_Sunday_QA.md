**Intro Copy：**
Every week, our team gets the same questions in DMs, emails, and at events. Here are the five we hear most — and the honest answers, no marketing spin.

---

### Q1: Do I really need an eye cream?

**A:** Short answer — yes, but probably not for the reason you think. The skin around your eyes is roughly 30% thinner than the rest of your face. The same actives that work on your cheeks can be too much for the orbital area. A dedicated eye cream isn't about marketing — it's formulated for the difference.

→ [Peptide Complex Eye Cream](https://depology.com/products/peptide-complex-wrinkle-defense-eye-cream)

---

### Q2: Can I use peptides AND retinol together?

**A:** Yes — and they often work better together. Peptides support the structure underneath; retinol speeds the surface renewal. The trick is layering in the right order (lighter to heavier) and giving your skin a few weeks to find the rhythm. If you're using a retinol product like our upgraded M1 patches, layer your peptide serum first.

---

### Q3: How do I know if my barrier is damaged?

**A:** Three signs to watch for: tightness right after washing, redness that doesn't calm down, and products that suddenly start to sting when they didn't before. Your barrier is the first thing to fix — until it's working, no active ingredient can really do its job.

→ [Triple Lipid + Q10 Moisturizing Treatment RICH](https://depology.com/products/triple-lipid-q10-revive-moisturizing-treatment-rich)

---

### Q4: What's the right order to apply products?

**A:** Lightest to heaviest. Cleanse → toner (if you use one) → serum → eye cream → moisturizer → SPF (AM) or face oil (PM). Skip a step? Fine. Out of order? You'll lose absorption. The order matters more than the number of steps.

---

### Q5: How young is too young to start anti-aging?

**A:** Mid-20s is a fine starting point — not because you have wrinkles, but because prevention is easier than reversal. SPF every morning + a gentle peptide serum is enough. The 10-step routine isn't necessary, even at 50.

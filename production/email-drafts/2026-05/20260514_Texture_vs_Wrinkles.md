**Body Copy（3 段）：**
Paragraph 1: When skin looks "older," half the time it's not actually wrinkles. It's texture — uneven surface, larger-looking pores, dullness — the surface signature of skin that's lost its renewal pace.

Paragraph 2: Wrinkles need peptides — formulas that support the structure underneath. Texture needs renewal — gentle refreshing that lifts away dead cells and smooths the surface. Most routines focus on one and ignore the other.

Paragraph 3: A complete mature-skin routine treats both, in the right order: refresh first, then layer the structure-builders. Skip step one and the peptides have less to work with.

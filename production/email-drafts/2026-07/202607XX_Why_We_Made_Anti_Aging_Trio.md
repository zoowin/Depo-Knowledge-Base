### Mechanism 1
> **The Delivery Gap → Microdart Patches**
> Standard creams stop at the surface. Around delicate eye zones — where retinol irritation is highest — that surface ceiling is exactly what limits results. Microdart delivery bypasses it. The patches do what the creams in your routine structurally can't.
> *Component: Deepcare+® Micro Dart Patches (Upgraded with Retinol, 8 pairs)*

### Mechanism 2
> **The Structural Gap → Matrixyl® 3000 Serum**
> Texture and elasticity aren't quick wins — they're a slow daily conversation with collagen synthesis. Palmitoyl peptides support that conversation when you apply them every morning, not when you remember every other week. The Serum is the long-game layer.
> *Component: Matrixyl® 3000 Collagen Serum*

### Mechanism 3
> **The Expression Gap → Argireline™ MPS Serum**
> Fine movement lines around the eyes, forehead, and mouth start showing before static wrinkles do — and standard peptide creams don't target them specifically. The 10% Argireline serum is built for the expression layer. Daily peptide, daily zone.
> *Component: Argireline™ MPS Serum*

## Three Principles Block（核心专题区块）
> 📐 对应 Klaviyo 位置：Body 下方专用 principle card 区域（3 张 principle card / 单列布局）

### Principle 1
> **Barrier First, Actives Second**
> The Western default was "use the strongest active you can tolerate." Korean derms reversed it: stabilize the lipid barrier first, then introduce actives gently. The barrier is the substrate everything else works on.
> *Translates to: lipid-rich PM cream as the foundation, not the afterthought*

### Principle 2
> **Delivery Matters as Much as Ingredient**
> Korean dermatology developed microneedle and microdart delivery systems years before the West adopted them. Same ingredient, different delivery = different result. The conversation isn't only "what's in it" — it's "how does it get there."
> *Translates to: targeted patches and microdart formats for delicate zones*

### Principle 3
> **Layering Beats Stacking Strength**
> Instead of one strong product, Korean routines layer multiple gentle steps — serum, essence, cream, occlusive. Each layer has a job. The whole stack is more effective than any single high-concentration product.
> *Translates to: serum + peptide cream + lipid seal — the layered PM stack*

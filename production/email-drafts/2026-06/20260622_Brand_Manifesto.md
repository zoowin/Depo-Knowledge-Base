## Body Section
> 📐 对应 Klaviyo 位置：Hero 下方 Text Block — 这是邮件的主体，长篇 manifesto

**Body Headline：**
What We Won't Say.

**Body Copy（3 段 — Brand Manifesto 例外，~580 字符）：**
Paragraph 1: We could write "younger in 7 days." We could write "erases wrinkles." Other brands do. The copy is easy. The traffic it pulls is real. But every time we've considered it, we've stopped — because the language doesn't survive contact with how skin actually works.

Paragraph 2: Mature skin doesn't reverse. It evolves. Peptides support collagen synthesis, they don't manufacture youth. Retinol changes cellular turnover patterns, it doesn't erase decades. Lipid barriers can be rebuilt, but not into the barrier you had at 30. The honest version is less catchy. We've decided we can live with that.

Paragraph 3: So here's what we will say. We make formulas backed by real ingredient science. We tell you what shifts, not what disappears. We respect that your skin at 55 has earned every line. And we believe the customers who want this version of skincare are the ones who stay.

## Brand Position Block（核心 manifesto 区块）
> 📐 对应 Klaviyo 位置：Body 下方 typography-led promise grid（双列 2×2 layout）

### What We Won't Do
> ❌ Promise miracle timeframes
> ❌ Compare ourselves to injectables or medical procedures
> ❌ Use before/after photos we can't verify
> ❌ Hide what an ingredient actually does

### What We Will Do
> ✅ Tell you what's in the formula and why
> ✅ Show ingredient mechanisms in plain language
> ✅ Build routines that respect how skin actually behaves
> ✅ Refund anything that doesn't work for you

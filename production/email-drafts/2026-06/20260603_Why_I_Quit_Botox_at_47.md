## Mandatory Compliance Disclaimer（M2 retinol 必须放邮件底部 small text）

> **A note on Retinol:** Apply sunscreen during daytime — retinol can increase sensitivity to UV. If you're new to retinol, your skin may need a short adjustment period (mild redness or slight peeling can be normal at first; this typically subsides within a few uses). Sensitive skin: start 2-3 times per week and gradually increase to nightly use. Not recommended during pregnancy, breastfeeding, or for those with a known retinol allergy.

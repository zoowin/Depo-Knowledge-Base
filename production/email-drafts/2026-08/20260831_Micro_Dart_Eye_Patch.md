- ✔ Wear sunscreen during daytime use

### Retinol Patch Use Note
Use on clean, dry skin and avoid touching the micro-darts. If new to retinol or sensitive, start 2–3 nights per week and increase gradually as skin is comfortable. An adjustment period with mild redness or slight peeling may occur at first. Use sunscreen during the day. On patch nights, avoid other retinoid products, high-strength AHA/BHA, and pure vitamin C around the same area. Discontinue use if irritation persists. Do not use if you are allergic to hydrocolloid.

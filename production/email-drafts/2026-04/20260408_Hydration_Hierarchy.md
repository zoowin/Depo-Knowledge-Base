## Body Section
> 📐 对应 Klaviyo 位置：Hero 下方 Text Block

**Body Headline：**
Wet Isn't Hydrated.

**Body Copy（3 段）：**
Paragraph 1: Splashing water on your face feels refreshing. But within minutes, your skin can feel tighter than before. That's evaporation — not hydration.
Paragraph 2: True hydration works in layers: attract moisture (humectants), nourish the barrier (emollients), and seal it in (occlusives). Skip a layer, and the others lose their effect.
Paragraph 3: The right routine doesn't just add water — it teaches your skin to hold onto it. That's where smart formulation makes all the difference.

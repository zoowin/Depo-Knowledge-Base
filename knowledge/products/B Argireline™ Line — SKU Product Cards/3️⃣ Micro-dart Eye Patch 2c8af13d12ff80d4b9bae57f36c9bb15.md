### **4. Hero Technology & Why**

#### 🌟 The Star Upgrade
- **Retinol 3,300 IU/g (~0.1% w/w)** — clinically meaningful mid-strength concentration. Strong enough for visible smoothing in 4-8 weeks, gentle enough for delicate eye area when buffered by support matrix.

#### 🚀 Delivery System (核心差异化)
- **Hydrocolloid Microdarts** — 微针绕过角质层，直达真皮浅层。Retinol 不在皮肤表面停留 = 传统 retinol 的"刺激入口"被消除。
- 这是为什么 M1 升级后能做到"daily wear on eye area" 而传统 retinol 不行。

#### 🛡️ Buffer Matrix（缓冲系统 — 为什么不刺激）
- **Madecassoside (Cica)** — 抗炎、缓和 retinol 反应
- **Anemarrhena Asphodeloides Root Extract**（韩方植物）— 舒缓抗炎补充
- **Sodium Hyaluronate + Trehalose + Glycerin**（三层保湿）— 缓冲 retinol 干燥

#### 🧬 Peptide Co-Active Stack
- **Argireline™ (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8)** — 表情纹（动态纹）放松
- **Matrixyl® / Pal-KTTKS (Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4)** — 支持胶原合成
- **GHK-Cu (Copper Tripeptide-1)** — 修复肽，与 retinol 协同
- **Arginine/Lysine Polypeptide** — 修护辅助

#### ✨ Antioxidant + Stability
- **Glutathione** — 主抗氧化剂，提亮 + 稳定 retinol 防氧化
- **Glycine soja (Soybean) Oil** — 植物雌激素 + 抗氧化 + retinol 稳定基质
- **Adenosine** — FDA-recognized 抗老活性，平滑细纹

### **6. How to Use**

1. Apply to cleansed and dry skin
2. Remove patches from film, **avoid touching the micro darts**
3. Apply patches and gently press to enhance absorption
4. Leave on for **at least 2 hours** (or **overnight for best results**)

**Frequency:**
- New to retinol or sensitive skin: **start 2-3 times per week**, gradually increase to nightly use
- Experienced retinol users: nightly use OK from start

**⚠️ Mandatory consumer education:**
- **Apply sunscreen during daytime** — retinol increases UV sensitivity
- **Adjustment period possible** — mild redness or slight peeling can be normal at first; typically subsides within a few uses
- Discontinue use if irritation persists

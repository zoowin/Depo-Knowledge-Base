### **6. Format & How to Use**

- **规格：** 1 box = **4 patches**（约 2 周用量）
- **用法：**
  1. Apply to cleansed and dry skin
  2. 移除离型膜，**避免触碰微针**
  3. 贴敷于额头，中心轻按几秒增强贴合
  4. **Leave on for at least 1 hour**, then remove
- **Frequency:** **Every 2–3 nights**（官网建议）

**⚠️ Mandatory consumer education（任何提及 retinol 的邮件必放）：**
- **Apply sunscreen during daytime** — retinol increases UV sensitivity
- **Adjustment period possible** — mild redness or slight peeling can be normal at first; typically subsides within a few uses
- Sensitive skin: start at the lower end of frequency; discontinue if irritation persists

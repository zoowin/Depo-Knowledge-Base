### **5. Formula Architecture**

#### **A. Hydration Phase**

- **Glycerin, Butylene Glycol, Propanediol, Glycereth-26, Trehalose:** Humectants that help attract and retain water at the skin surface
- **Sodium Hyaluronate:** Moisture-binding humectant that supports a plumper, smoother appearance
- **Rosa Damascena Flower Water:** Botanical water with skin-conditioning and aromatic properties

#### **B. Nourishing Emollient Phase**

- **Neopentyl Glycol Diheptanoate, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Pentaerythrityl Tetraethylhexanoate:** Lightweight emollients that improve slip and softness
- **Squalane:** Skin-compatible emollient that helps reduce dry, tight feel
- **Olive Fruit Oil, Jojoba Seed Oil, Shea Butter:** Nourishing lipids that support softness and comfort

#### **C. Comfort + Barrier-Support System**

- **Panthenol, Allantoin, Dipotassium Glycyrrhizate:** Skin-conditioning ingredients that help promote a calm, comfortable feel
- **Ceramide NP, Cholesterol, Phytosphingosine, Acetylphytosphingosine:** Barrier-associated lipids that support moisture retention and skin comfort
- **Hydrogenated Lecithin:** Skin-conditioning lipid and formula stabilizer

#### **D. Mature-Skin Appearance Support**

- **Niacinamide:** Supports a more even, radiant appearance and the look of a healthy moisture barrier
- **Adenosine:** Skin-conditioning ingredient used in formulas targeting the appearance of visible lines
- **Collagen:** Film-forming and moisturizing ingredient that supports a smoother skin feel
- **Multi-peptide blend:** Includes Acetyl Hexapeptide-8, Acetyl Octapeptide-3, Copper Tripeptide-1, Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1, Palmitoyl Tripeptide-5, Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4, and additional peptides for mature-skin positioning

#### **E. Featured Extracts + Botanical Oils**

- **Caviar Extract — 45 ppm (0.0045%):** Present as a featured formula ingredient. Keep language proportional; do not describe the formula as caviar-rich or high-strength caviar care.
- **Tuber Melanosporum Extract:** Black truffle extract used as a skin-conditioning botanical; do not assign lifting or firming claims to this ingredient without substantiation.
- **Lithospermum Erythrorhizon Root Extract:** Botanical skin-conditioning extract
- **Tanacetum Annuum, Orange Peel, Patchouli, Geranium, Clove, Chamomile, and Black Pepper Oils:** Aromatic botanical oils that contribute skin-conditioning and sensorial properties

### **7. How to Use**

1. Shake the bottle **3–5 times** to mix the two layers.
2. Hold the bottle upright, approximately **15–20 cm** from the face.
3. Close the eyes and mist an appropriate amount over the face.
4. Pat gently to help the formula settle and absorb.

**Routine placement:**

- **AM / PM:** After cleansing, use as a toner-serum mist before treatment serums and moisturizer
- **Over makeup:** Mist lightly when skin needs a refreshed, more hydrated appearance
- **Anytime:** Use as a lightweight moisture refresh during the day

**Storage:** Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
**Caution:** For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Keep out of reach of children.
